back home

Born in Indirapuram, raised and educated in Odisha for 9 years, then Gujarat for another 9, now studying in Kerala (i'm pan indian). I remember my first lucid memory is booting a computer my dad had for my elder cousin. Even though I had a computer it didn't have internet. So most of my time was spent playing Road Rash, pitfall, Zuma; games. No one in my family is a programmer or even remotely in tech, so I was never introduced to that world irl. It was when I got internet that my world began developing. But even there, most of my time was spent on platforms like kogama playing online games making friends who lived far away.

Then one day I figured that you could actually make your own games on kogama!!. I didn't end up making a front page game, but I remember that excitement of building something I imagine. I made a few buildings, I had the idea planned "gta but kogama" (i didn't have gta and wanted to play it). And I tried and I couldn't do it alone. I don't remember the outcome, I probably left it to play fifa (new thing i discovered). I am like little cheerful kid in sandals that thinks of our world as many chocolate fountains. I run around dipping my finger into them. If I got access to them, and didn't shy away. But somehow, the fountains I dipped my fingers all led to me pursuing computer science.

I'd say I like vim, zsh, nixos, history, security, consciousness, religion, freedom, my spotify favourites, no bullshitism, mangas, anime, talking to people. If weather is nice I'd say yes to go anywhere with you waterfall, morning jogs, hiking.

I think I have consumed enough entertainment content to entertain a village in 1900s, and I'm actively cutting down it, but movies and literature get a pass. Sometimes my code makes it to my github profile.

Lately I've been active on twitch streaming whatever I'm doing so maybe you can hmu there ig. Twitch
