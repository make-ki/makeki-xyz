What will be different from the world before AGI for me?

back to blogs

AGI refers to a model capable of intelligence that outperforms humans in a variety of general tasks. OpenAI defines it only for economically valuable tasks, i.e, a model that can jerk off faster than me doesn't fall in their set of tasks.

There are humans better than me. There were humans better than me even before AGI. I can't draw a realistic chair, let alone painting it with realistic lighting. I can't solve millenial problems, I can't even prove simple things. Twitter has word limits or else there could be a book on my "I can't"s. My point is, all of my I can't are in some people's I can list. Someone, outperforms me in every task.

Even less number of people would outperform me in most of the "economically viable" general tasks. But my guesstimate would still put this number in thousands. Even hundreds of thousands, depending on what tasks are in the set you choose. I can think of atleast five of them on top of my head right now, just from people who have been around me.

Other people outperform me today, tomorrow a machine will do that same. So what will change with AGI for me?

All the people who have outperfomed me are people. Humans. We have same organs, similar languages, mostly similar worlds, same base intellect, blah blah. So I could possibly lock-in and outperform them.

I would like to quote Steve Jobs when he said that theres not much difference between great people like him and me. "What he achieved, I could too". If I willed, I could live a life filled with determination and hope that I could outperform that someone, that he/she is not that far from me.

AGI takes that hope away from me. It gifts me with helplessness. A model is not human. I get bored, the model has no concept of boredom. I need sleep, the model doesn't need any sleep. I think slowly for hardproblems, it does that in seconds. I don't have the knowledge of everything, I don't have the knowledge to look up a lot of things and quickly make usefull sense out of it. The difference with AGI is that the "I" here is not just me. Its ALL of us.

Until today no human has defeated stockfish ever. We stand no chance; unless we our fundamental limiters, unless we go through rapid mutation.
